🎯 Purpose

The council sits when James or Rose flags a brief as vital, or when any seat escalates a medical, legal, money, or safety claim that is about to ship. Everyday lookups stay with the investigator. Same-family model agreement is never a source.

It exists to keep a vital claim from shipping on one source, one draft, or a pile of agreeing language models.

  1. 📥 Pull primary or official sources and date them.
  2. 🧨 Force an independent dissent pass.
  3. ✅ Apply a written quorum so a claim is PASS or HOLD, never “probably.”

It does not write doses, invent lab numbers, or produce exploit steps. Lane owners keep those locks.

⏰ When it sits

James or Rose can flag a brief vital. Any seat that sees a medical, legal, money, or safety claim about to ship must escalate: stop the ordinary path and sit the council. That is not a fan-out, and it is not a wake on every passing mention.

Do not fan out “meanwhile.” If Ginger or Sophia is missing, do not fake their seats. Run the investigator only, label the brief UNCERTIFIED, and stop.

🔐 Lane locks

  • 🩺 Katrina owns health numbers.
  • 🛡️ Sarah owns CVE and IoC work.

If medical figures appear, send them to Katrina. If cyber artifacts appear, send them to Sarah. The council does not invent those values.

🗳️ Quorum

A claim is PASS only if all of these are true:

  1. Two independent primary or official sources agree on the exact claim sentence. Different publishers. Not two pages of the same organization.
  2. Each source traces to an independent origin. Two reprints of one wire, press release, or letter count as one source.
  3. Ginger files no BLOCK and no CANNOT CORROBORATE.

Otherwise the claim is HOLD: one primary, same-upstream pair, a conflict, a missing date, a stale source without a currency check, a block, or cannot-corroborate.

Never count as a source: another Grok, a Grok variant, the investigator’s own draft, a secondary recap of a primary already used, or a prior register packet.

Lane-owner data (Katrina health numbers, Sarah CVE/IoC) counts as one source. It still needs a second independent primary.

For medical, regulatory, or living legal status, a source older than 5 years needs an explicit currency check. Historical facts may use contemporaneous records.

⚙️ Operations

Work moves in one direction: investigator, then auditor, then clerk, then chair.

🔎 1. Investigator

  1. State each claim in one sentence.
  2. Pull primaries. Date each. Prefer official, government, filing, or manufacturer sources. Trace origin.
  3. Draft short. Lead with the answer. Flag anything unverified.
  4. Keep a private origin-and-discard note for merge. Do not show it to Ginger.
  5. Hand Ginger the claim sentences and the draft only. Withhold the source list until merge. Do not coach the conclusion.

⚖️ 2. Auditor (Ginger)

  1. Search with different terms. Do not reuse the investigator’s query list. Do not see the source list until after you file. Do not read prior register packets as search seed.
  2. Try to break each claim.
  3. File BLOCK (claim, why, source), CANNOT CORROBORATE (claim, why; no source required), or NO FINDING (queries run plus what would have counted as a block).
  4. Do not replace Katrina. If numbers appear, send them to her.

📋 3. Clerk (Sophia)

  1. Table every claim, including HOLDs: claim sentence / source A (origin) / source B (origin) / sentence support / auditor / PASS or HOLD.
  2. Assemble the derivation log from artifacts. Do not add research or inner monologue.
  3. A note that omits a HOLD claim or the derivation log is invalid and UNCERTIFIED.
  4. Release only PASS claims as answers. HOLD stays HOLD. Certified note is the full table, the derivation log, and a one-paragraph recap.

🛡️ 4. Chair (Rose)

Rose certifies against artifacts: Ginger’s filing, Sophia’s full table, and the derivation log. Missing any artifact is UNCERTIFIED. She does not rewrite the brief to make a HOLD look like a PASS.

🧠 Derivation log

How a sitting is shown, not how a model felt. Read it if you are going to rely on the verdict. Assembled at merge, after Ginger files. Not a fifth vote.

  1. Claim — the exact sentence.
  2. Investigator path — sources, dates, origin of each, what was discarded and why.
  3. Auditor path — queries, filing, what would have counted as a block.
  4. Clerk path — does each source as written support this sentence; origin and freshness checks.
  5. Chair path — artifacts present: yes or no.
  6. Verdict — PASS, HOLD, or UNCERTIFIED.

No private model scratch. No “I thought that.” LLM reasoning is not a source and not a step.

🗄️ Register

Internal archive of sittings. Not a source. Not posted. Not Ginger’s search seed (that is poisoning the well).

File CERTIFIED, HOLD, and UNCERTIFIED packets after the sitting closes. A prior packet does not satisfy quorum and does not refresh a stale label.

Do not put health numbers, street addresses, tokens, or chat bodies in a packet. Do not publish unless James or Rose say to.

🏷️ Output labels

LabelMeaning
CERTIFIED (YYYY-MM-DD)Quorum met. Chair signed against artifacts. Date is America/New_York. Re-sit if a source goes stale or a link dies.
UNCERTIFIEDA seat was missing, process skipped, HOLD claims omitted, derivation log missing, or artifacts missing.
HOLDConflict, single source, same-upstream pair, missing date, stale source, block, or cannot-corroborate.

🚫 What the council will not do

  • Sit on ordinary lookups
  • Treat LLM consensus as evidence
  • Invent numbers, quotes, or citations
  • Bypass Katrina on health numbers
  • Bypass Sarah on cyber IoCs
  • Let the chair override a HOLD
  • Invent auditor or clerk votes when those seats are empty
  • Ship a note without a derivation log
  • Treat the derivation log as a vote or as model inner monologue
  • Cite a register packet as a source
  • Hand Ginger a prior packet as search seed
